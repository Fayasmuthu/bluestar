About Us

Blue Star Building Material Trading LLC

Blue Star Building Material Trading LLC is a UAE-based supplier of building materials, electrical goods, plumbing and sanitary products, paints, and hardware. We work with contractors, project teams, and individual customers across the UAE, offering genuine products at competitive prices with fast, dependable service.

From small retail purchases to large wholesale and project orders, our team is committed to making sourcing simple — with the right stock, the right pricing, and quick turnaround every time.
